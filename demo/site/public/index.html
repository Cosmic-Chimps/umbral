Umbral

Sign in without a password, and see exactly what the app gets.

This is a public, throwaway instance of Umbral, a self-hosted OpenID Connect provider. Sign in to a small sample app and it shows you the claims Umbral sent it. That is the whole demo.

Try signing in What happens to your data

Five ways in

GitHub, Google, Discord, a Mastodon or GoToSocial account, or a passkey. A passkey needs no account anywhere: create one on the sign-in page, on your phone or your laptop. With a Mastodon or GoToSocial account, you type your handle and your own server asks for your consent.

What you will see

  1. The sample app sends you to Umbral to sign in.
  2. Umbral's sign-in page offers the five ways. Pick one.
  3. You are back in the app, on a page that lists the claims it received: sub, name, idp, and email with email_verified when the provider shared an address.

Cancel at the provider and you land back on the sign-in page. Sign out and the app forgets you.

Everything here is wiped every night at 04:00 UTC, accounts included. The instance is off for about a minute while that happens.

How it runs

One container with an embedded SQLite database, behind a reverse proxy, deployed from an Aspire AppHost with the Umbral hosting package. The admin area is not part of the demo; the documentation shows it.
